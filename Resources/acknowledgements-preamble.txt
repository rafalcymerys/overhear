SPEECH AND WAKE WORD MODELS

Overhear is compatible with a variety of transcription and wake word models.
The models are not distributed as a part of the application, however, they may
be downloaded on demand through the application's user interface.

Whisper (tiny, base, base.en, small, large-v3-turbo)
    Core ML conversions published at argmaxinc/whisperkit-coreml.
    MIT licensed, as are OpenAI's original weights.

Parakeet TDT 0.6B v2 and v3
    Trained and published by NVIDIA as nvidia/parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v2 and
    nvidia/parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3, used here in the Core ML conversions
    published at FluidInference/parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v2-coreml and
    FluidInference/parakeet-tdt-0.6b-v3-coreml.
    Licensed CC BY 4.0. Credit to NVIDIA Corporation.
    https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/

Wake words, and the shared melspectrogram and embedding models
    From David Scripka's openWakeWord, release v0.5.1.
    The project's code is Apache-2.0; its pre-trained models — all of them,
    including the two shared feature models — are licensed
    CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Credit to David Scripka.
    https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/
    https://github.com/dscripka/openWakeWord
